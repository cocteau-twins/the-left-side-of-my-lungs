# the-left-side-of-my-lungs


One side of me whispers to stay. Here, in

the abyss, there is no need for light. The

shadows cradle me, a refuge from the

weight of seeking. But another side stirs

me, restlessly, telling me to walk forward,

to climb, to see. Only by leaving the abyss

will I taste the horizon, only through

motion can I find meaning. The light is no

promise, yet it calls me, a soft, aching lure

pulling me to what I do not know. You, my

lover, you call me to suffering, as if love

could only bloom in wounds. But this

hollow shell, once fragile, once shattered,

has endured enough pain. It craves

pleasure and it hungers for joy. I am torn,

a wanderer caught between the stillness

and the unknown. One side fears the cost

of light. The other cannot bear to linger in

shadow. And so I wait, suspended in the

void, neither falling nor flying, yearning for

wholeness in the fracture of myself.
